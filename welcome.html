What is metldr?

metldr is a free, open source agent that lives in your browser. It reads pages, pulls out data and does the clicking for you. Everything it changes waits for your approval.

  • Works beside you

    Fills forms, clicks through pages and finishes tasks in its own tab.

  • Asks first

    Nothing on a page changes until you approve it. Stop always works.

  • Pulls out data

    Turns any page into a table or list you can sort, edit and copy.

  • Answers you can use

    Charts, tables and checklists right in the chat.

  • Reads with sources

    Summaries of pages, PDFs and Gmail threads, linked to where they came from.

  • One shortcut

    Open it from the toolbar or with Alt Shift M.
